Traditional approaches vs. VMI

✗ Traditional Approaches
✗

What you get

A data connection, bundled add-on, or commercial product. The state fills the gaps.

✗

Pricing

Commercial pricing that rises over time. True cost often hidden inside larger contracts.

✗

Data quality

Raw data delivered as-is. Incomplete or unusable records reach caseworkers.

✗

Open source

Proprietary. No visibility into how the system works.

✗

Vendor lock-in

High. Switching means rebuilding from scratch.

✗

Accessibility

State inherits responsibility for all embedded third-party components.

✗

Service model

Per-verification billing regardless of eligibility value.

✓ VMI — Digital Public Works
✓

What you get

A complete verification service. Consent, data validation, and caseworker-ready reports included.

✓

Pricing

Nonprofit, at cost. Price decreases as more states join the platform.

✓

Data quality

Every report passes programmatic validation. Insufficient records are blocked before reaching a caseworker.

✓

Open source

Full codebase published under AGPL-3.0 on GitHub.

✓

Vendor lock-in

None. States retain full access to code, architecture, and documentation.

✓

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA with independent third-party auditing funded by AARP Foundation.

✓

Service model

Embedded partnership: discovery sprints, case reviews, ongoing service design.
